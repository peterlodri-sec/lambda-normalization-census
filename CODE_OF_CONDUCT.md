# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards
Examples of positive behavior: empathy and kindness, respecting differing viewpoints, graceful acceptance of constructive feedback, focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable: harassment, trolling, insults, publishing others' private information, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement
Report unacceptable behavior to **cabotage@pm.me**. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly; reporter privacy is respected. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviors deemed inappropriate.

Full text and enforcement guidelines: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
